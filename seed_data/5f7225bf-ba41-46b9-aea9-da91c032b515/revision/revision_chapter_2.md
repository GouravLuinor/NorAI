# Revision Notes — Chapter 2: Why Universities Study Their Past

## Key Exam Takeaways

- Small colleges across the U.S. are increasingly investigating their historical connections to the institution of slavery.
- The 'Legacies of Slavery' project is culminating in a final conference in Memphis, Tennessee.
- The course is a condensed version of a larger Civil War and Reconstruction curriculum, focused on explaining modern political polarization and institutional dysfunction.
- History is presented as containing an inherent element of human tragedy that is relevant to all students.

## Core Concepts Breakdown

- **Legacies of Slavery**: A research initiative involving small colleges investigating their historical ties to slavery.
- **Institutional Accountability**: The process by which universities examine their past actions and connections to historical injustices like slavery.
- **Historical Context**: Using the study of the Civil War and Reconstruction to better understand contemporary political polarization and institutional dysfunction.
