# Revision Notes — Chapter 1: Introduction to the Course and the Yale Slavery Project

## Key Exam Takeaways

- The course is based on the Yale Slavery Research Project, which investigates Yale's historical entanglements with slavery and racial ideology.
- The research begins in the 17th century, specifically analyzing the impact of the Pequot War and King Philip's War in Connecticut.
- The narrative concludes in 1915, notably covering the history of the Yale War Memorial.
- Yale's project is part of a wider movement of approximately 90 universities investigating their historical ties to slavery.

## Core Concepts Breakdown

- **Yale Slavery Research Project**: A research initiative led by the lecturer to uncover Yale University's historical connections to slavery and racial ideology.
- **Institutional History**: The study of how universities, like Yale, have been shaped by and entangled with broader historical forces such as slavery.
- **Pequot War and King Philip's War**: Two major 17th-century conflicts in Connecticut that serve as the starting point for the university's historical investigation.
