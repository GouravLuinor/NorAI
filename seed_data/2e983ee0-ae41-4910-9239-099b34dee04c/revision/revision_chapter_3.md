# Revision Notes — Chapter 3: Behavioral Finance and the Subprime Crisis

## Key Exam Takeaways

- Behavioral finance integrates traditional finance with psychology, sociology, anthropology, and political science.
- Subprime mortgages are loans given to borrowers with poor credit history, posing a high risk of default.
- The subprime crisis illustrates how housing market declines lead to widespread defaults and institutional instability.
- Finance can be conceptualized as a technology or an engineering device with complex, detailed structures.

## Core Concepts Breakdown

- **Behavioral Finance**: A field that combines finance theory with social sciences like psychology to better understand market behavior.
- **Subprime Mortgage**: A mortgage loan provided to a borrower with a poor credit history, indicating a higher risk of default.
- **Finance as Technology**: The view that financial instruments are complex, engineered devices designed to perform specific functions.
