# Revision Notes — Chapter 1: Course Introduction and Logistics

## Key Exam Takeaways

- Finance is a fundamental, global technology that underpins most human endeavors, making this course relevant beyond just those pursuing finance careers.
- The course is designed as an intellectual exploration of how financial markets function in the real world, rather than a purely vocational program.
- Economics 252 (Financial Markets) and Economics 251 (Financial Theory) are self-contained courses; Economics 251 is more mathematically intensive.
- The course will involve significant detail and factual learning, which is necessary to understand the practical operations of financial markets.

## Core Concepts Breakdown

- **Financial Markets**: The subject of this course, viewed as a powerful, foundational technology that influences society and human endeavors globally.
- **Behavioral Finance**: The application of psychological insights to the study of financial markets and decision-making.
- **Financial Theory vs. Markets**: A distinction between two Yale courses where Financial Theory focuses more on mathematical and theoretical detail, while Financial Markets emphasizes real-world application.
