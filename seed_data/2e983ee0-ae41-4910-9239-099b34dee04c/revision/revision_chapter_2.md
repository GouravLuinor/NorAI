# Revision Notes — Chapter 2: Course Materials and Resources

## Key Exam Takeaways

- Master all 'key terms' and 'key points' in the Fabozzi et al. textbook.
- Clickable links on the reading list are mandatory; library reserve books are optional.
- Grading is weighted: 10% problem sets, 20% midterm 1, 30% midterm 2, 40% final.
- Attend TA sections to review problem sets and engage with course material.

## Core Concepts Breakdown

- **Textbook Requirements**: Students must read Fabozzi et al., Siegel's 'Stocks for the Long Run', and Shiller's 'Irrational Exuberance', focusing on key terms and points.
- **Grading Policy**: The final grade is determined by a weighted combination of problem sets (10%), two midterms (20% and 30%), and a final exam (40%).
- **TA Sections**: Weekly sections are held to review problem sets and provide additional support to students in this large course.
