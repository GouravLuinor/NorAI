# Revision Notes — Chapter 2: Competitive Landscape and Architectural Strategies

## Key Exam Takeaways

- AI competition is driving a split between massive cloud-native models and agile, efficient, smaller-scale architectures.
- Post-training is a high-leverage strategy that allows existing base models to achieve frontier-level performance without full retraining.
- Terminal Bench is a critical metric for evaluating the agentic and technical capabilities of modern LLMs.
- Google's dual-track strategy reflects the need to balance long-term frontier research with immediate cost-efficiency in large-scale search applications.

## Core Concepts Breakdown

- **Post-training**: The process of refining a pre-trained base model to improve performance on specific tasks without changing the underlying base architecture.
- **Terminal Bench**: A specialized benchmark used to evaluate an LLM's ability to perform agentic tasks within a command-line interface environment.
- **Bifurcated Strategy**: A business approach where a company simultaneously pursues two distinct paths, such as developing both massive frontier models and lightweight efficient models.
