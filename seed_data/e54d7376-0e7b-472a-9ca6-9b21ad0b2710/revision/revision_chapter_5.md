# Revision Notes — Chapter 5: Profit, Loss, and Resource Reallocation

## Key Exam Takeaways

- Profits and losses act as objective feedback signals that guide the efficient allocation of scarce resources.
- Business failure is a necessary market process that reallocates resources from low-value to high-value sectors.
- Government subsidies and 'too big to fail' policies distort market signals, leading to systemic inefficiency.
- Wages are determined by the value an employee creates for an employer, not by the difficulty of the work.

## Core Concepts Breakdown

- **Market Signaling**: The use of price and profit/loss data to communicate where resources are most needed in an economy.
- **Resource Reallocation**: The process of shifting capital, labor, and time from unproductive or low-value uses to high-value uses.
- **Marginal Revenue Product**: The economic value created by an additional unit of labor, which serves as the upper bound for wage determination.
