# Revision Notes — Chapter 1: Foundations of Economic Thinking: Incentives and Opportunity Cost

## Key Exam Takeaways

- Economics is the study of resource allocation under the constraint of scarcity.
- Opportunity cost is the value of the foregone alternative, not just the monetary cost.
- Static revenue projections fail because they ignore how taxpayers change their behavior in response to tax rate changes.
- Government interventions like price controls often lead to shortages by disrupting market equilibrium.

## Core Concepts Breakdown

- **Opportunity Cost**: The value of the next best alternative given up when making a choice.
- **Capital Flight**: The movement of assets or money from one jurisdiction to another, often triggered by unfavorable tax or economic policies.
- **Incentive Structures**: The system of rewards and penalties that motivates individuals to act in specific ways.
- **Static Revenue Projection**: A flawed fiscal planning method that assumes tax base behavior remains constant despite changes in tax rates.
