# Revision Notes — Chapter 6: Labor Economics: Value Creation and Wage Determination

## Key Exam Takeaways

- Wages are fundamentally tied to the Marginal Revenue Product of Labor (MRPL); hiring is only viable when the wage is less than or equal to the revenue generated.
- Trade is a non-zero-sum cooperative process that increases total utility through the reallocation of resources based on comparative advantage.
- Businesses respond to labor costs exceeding value output by substituting labor with capital (automation) or reducing operations.
- Personal income growth is best achieved by acquiring scarce, high-value skills rather than simply increasing time spent working.

## Core Concepts Breakdown

- **MRPL**: The additional revenue generated by employing one additional unit of labor.
- **Comparative Advantage**: The ability to produce a good or service at a lower opportunity cost than others.
- **Non-zero-sum game**: A situation where the total gains and losses of all participants sum to more than zero, allowing mutual benefit.
- **Capital Substitution**: The process of replacing human labor with technology or machinery when labor costs exceed the value produced.
