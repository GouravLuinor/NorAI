# Revision Notes — Chapter 4: Fiscal Policy and the Unintended Consequences of Taxation

## Key Exam Takeaways

- Price ceilings create shortages by suppressing prices below equilibrium; price floors create surpluses by inflating them above equilibrium.
- Price controls are regressive, often harming the poor who cannot bypass shortages through bribery or social capital.
- Profits signal successful resource allocation, while losses signal the need to pivot and stop wasting resources.
- The effectiveness of any fiscal policy should be measured by its impact on the supply and availability of goods.

## Core Concepts Breakdown

- **Price Ceiling**: A government-imposed maximum price below equilibrium that causes shortages.
- **Price Floor**: A government-imposed minimum price above equilibrium that causes surpluses.
- **Market Signals**: Profits and losses that guide producers on how to allocate resources based on consumer demand.
- **Economic Loss**: A signal that resources are being wasted on products that consumers do not value.
