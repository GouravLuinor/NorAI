# Revision Notes — Chapter 1: Course Overview and Logistics

## Key Exam Takeaways

- The course covers a broad range of physics from Newtonian mechanics to quantum mechanics and relativity.
- Homework is due every Wednesday before class, with solutions posted that same afternoon.
- The grading scheme is 20% homework, 30% midterm, and 50% final, with an amnesty plan that uses the higher of the weighted average or the final exam score.
- Support is available through TA-led discussion sections on Tuesdays, including a Tuesday night session for homework help.

## Core Concepts Breakdown

- **Amnesty Plan**: A grading policy where the final grade is the higher of the weighted course average or the final exam score.
- **Course Logistics**: The administrative framework including meeting times, homework submission procedures, and the use of the course website.
- **Physics Curriculum**: A comprehensive year-long sequence covering classical mechanics, special relativity, and quantum mechanics.
