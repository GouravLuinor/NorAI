# Revision Notes — Chapter 3: Q&A and Course Prerequisites

## Key Exam Takeaways

- Students are encouraged to ask questions freely during class to improve understanding and avoid distracting side conversations.
- Taking the lab concurrently is recommended because the lecture lacks in-class demonstrations.
- The course requires proficiency in basic differential calculus (derivatives, integrals) and trigonometry (sine, cosine, identities).
- The midterm is tentatively scheduled for around October 20th, covering approximately the first 12 lectures.

## Core Concepts Breakdown

- **Calculus Prerequisites**: Students must know how to differentiate and integrate elementary functions and understand the concept of derivatives.
- **Laboratory Importance**: Labs are essential for seeing the experimental basis of physics laws, as the lecture does not include demonstrations.
- **Midterm Coverage**: The midterm will cover approximately the first 12 of the 24 total lectures.
