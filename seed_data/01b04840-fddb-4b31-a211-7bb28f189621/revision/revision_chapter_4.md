# Revision Notes — Chapter 4: Example: Derivative of 1/x

## Key Exam Takeaways

- The difference quotient for $f(x) = 1/x$ is $\frac{1/(x_0 + \Delta x) - 1/x_0}{\Delta x}$.
- Algebraic simplification involves finding a common denominator and canceling $\Delta x$ to resolve the $0/0$ indeterminate form.
- The derivative of $1/x$ is $-1/x^2$.
- Plausibility checks confirm the result is negative and that the slope magnitude decreases as $x$ increases.

## Core Concepts Breakdown

- **Difference Quotient**: The ratio of the change in function value to the change in input, used to define the slope of a secant line.
- **Algebraic Simplification**: The process of manipulating an expression to remove indeterminate forms like 0/0, allowing for the evaluation of a limit.
- **Limit Calculation**: The process of finding the value a function approaches as the input variable gets arbitrarily close to a specific value.
- **Plausibility Check**: Evaluating whether a mathematical result makes sense by comparing it to geometric properties like slope sign and steepness.
