# Revision Notes — Chapter 6: Solving the Geometry Problem

## Key Exam Takeaways

- The tangent line to $y = 1/x$ at $(x_0, y_0)$ has the equation $y - y_0 = -1/x_0^2(x - x_0)$.
- The X-intercept is found by setting $y=0$, resulting in $x = 2x_0$.
- Symmetry around the diagonal $y=x$ allows us to determine the Y-intercept as $y = 2y_0$.
- The equation $xy=1$ is symmetric with respect to $x$ and $y$, justifying the shortcut.

## Core Concepts Breakdown

- **X-intercept**: The point where the tangent line crosses the x-axis, found by setting the y-coordinate to zero.
- **Y-intercept**: The point where the tangent line crosses the y-axis, found by setting the x-coordinate to zero.
- **Symmetry**: A property of the curve $y=1/x$ where swapping $x$ and $y$ leaves the equation unchanged, allowing for simplified calculations.
