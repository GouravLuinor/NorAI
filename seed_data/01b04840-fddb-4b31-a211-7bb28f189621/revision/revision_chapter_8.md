# Revision Notes — Chapter 8: Derivative of Power Functions

## Key Exam Takeaways

- The derivative of $x^n$ is $n x^{n-1}$ for positive integers $n$.
- The binomial theorem is used to expand $(x + \Delta x)^n$, keeping only the first two terms.
- Higher-order terms of $\Delta x$ (denoted as $O((\Delta x)^2)$) vanish when taking the limit as $\Delta x \to 0$.
- The power rule applies term-by-term to polynomials.

## Core Concepts Breakdown

- **Power Rule**: The formula $\frac{d}{dx} x^n = n x^{n-1}$ used to differentiate power functions.
- **Binomial Theorem**: A formula used to expand $(x + \Delta x)^n$ into a series of terms.
- **Higher Order Terms**: Terms in an expansion involving $(\Delta x)^2$ or higher powers that become zero in the limit.
