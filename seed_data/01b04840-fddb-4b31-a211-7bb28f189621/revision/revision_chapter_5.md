# Revision Notes — Chapter 5: Calculus in Context: A Word Problem

## Key Exam Takeaways

- Calculus problems in context are often routine calculus steps embedded in broader mathematical problems.
- The primary challenge in word problems is often the non-calculus algebra and geometry required to set up the problem.
- For the curve y = 1/x, the tangent line equation is the only part of the area problem requiring calculus.
- Consistent notation (e.g., using x0, y0 for a specific point) is essential for solving geometry-based calculus problems.

## Core Concepts Breakdown

- **Calculus in Context**: The idea that calculus is often a small, routine part of a larger word problem requiring various mathematical skills.
- **Tangent Line Equation**: The specific calculus step required to find the slope of a line touching a curve at a point (x0, y0).
- **Geometric Modeling**: Using variables and specific points to translate a curve-based problem into a standard geometry problem, such as finding triangle areas.
