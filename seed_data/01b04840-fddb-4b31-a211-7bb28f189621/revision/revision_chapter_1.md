# Revision Notes — Chapter 1: Introduction to Differentiation

## Key Exam Takeaways

- The derivative has both geometric and physical interpretations.
- Derivatives are fundamental to measurements in science, engineering, and economics.
- The geometric goal is to find the tangent line to a function $f(x)$ at a point $(x_0, y_0)$.
- The ultimate goal of the unit is to learn how to differentiate any function.

## Core Concepts Breakdown

- **Derivative**: A fundamental concept in calculus used to analyze functions, with applications across science and engineering.
- **Geometric Interpretation**: The visualization of the derivative as the slope of the tangent line to a function's graph at a specific point.
- **Tangent Line**: A line that touches a curve at a specific point $(x_0, y_0)$, representing the instantaneous behavior of the function there.
