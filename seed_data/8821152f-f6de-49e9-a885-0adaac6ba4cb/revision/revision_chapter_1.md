# Revision Notes — Chapter 1: Course Introduction and Goals

## Key Exam Takeaways

- The course focuses on solving, proving, and communicating computational problems.
- Correctness and efficiency are the two primary metrics for evaluating algorithmic solutions.
- Effective communication of algorithmic ideas to other people is as important as the solution itself.
- The course requires significant writing to justify and prove algorithmic claims.

## Core Concepts Breakdown

- **Computational Problems**: The primary subject matter that students will learn to solve throughout the course.
- **Correctness**: The requirement to prove that an algorithm produces the intended result for all inputs.
- **Efficiency**: The requirement to argue that a solution performs better than alternative approaches.
- **Communication**: The ability to clearly explain and justify algorithmic solutions to other people.
